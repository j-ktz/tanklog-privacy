TankLog: Aquascape (“TankLog”) is made by Chris Dominguez, who is responsible for your data (the “controller” under data protection law). Questions or requests: tanklog.support@gmail.com.

The short version

  • TankLog is free and works without an account. Your tanks are stored on your device.
  • Cloud Sync is optional and paid once. It keeps a copy of your tanks in your own iCloud, so they are backed up and the same on your devices. TankLog cannot see it.
  • The only thing we ever receive is feedback you choose to send us.
  • We never sell your data, share it or use it to market to you. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • You can export or delete your data at any time.

Your data on your device

Everything you enter (tanks, test results, logs, notes, schedules, livestock, plants, equipment, photos and settings) is stored on your device. We cannot see it.

  • Reminders are scheduled by iOS on your device. No server is involved.
  • Photos are added only when you choose them from your camera or photo library, and they stay in the app.
  • Safety copies: TankLog keeps a few automatic copies of your data in its private storage on your device, so a mistake can be undone. They never leave your device.
  • Device backups: like any app, TankLog’s data may be included in your iCloud or computer backups. Those are managed by Apple and you, not by us.
  • Export and import create files that you control.
  • TankLog contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking tools, and it contacts no server unless you use one of the features described below.

Cloud Sync (optional)

  • What is synced: your tanks and everything in them, including photos, and your app settings (language, units, theme and time format).
  • Where: in a private area of your own iCloud, under your Apple Account, using Apple’s CloudKit. It counts toward your iCloud storage. We have no servers for your tank data and cannot access your iCloud. Apple stores it under Apple’s Privacy Policy, encrypted in transit and on Apple’s servers.
  • Which iCloud account: so that data from two iCloud accounts is never mixed, TankLog keeps an anonymous ID that iCloud provides for TankLog alone. It stays on your device and we never receive it.
  • Why: only to back up your tanks and keep them the same on your devices.

How long: until you delete it.

  • Deleting a tank removes its contents and photos from your iCloud. A small marker (an ID and the time of deletion, with no content) stays so your other devices know to delete it too.
  • Settings → Cloud Sync → Delete TankLog data from iCloud removes everything TankLog stored in your iCloud, on all your devices. The tanks on your device stay.
  • Turning sync off pauses it. Your iCloud copy stays until you delete it.

Buying Cloud Sync

  • Apple processes the payment. We never see your card details or your Apple Account.
  • TankLog asks Apple, on your device, whether you have bought Cloud Sync, so it can unlock it. We receive no personal information from this; Apple gives developers only summary sales reports.
  • For a refund, use Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Sending feedback

  • Send Feedback sends the type of message, the subject, your message, the app version and, if you choose to add it, your email address, which we use only to reply to you. It does not send your name unless you type it. The form runs through Formspree (USA), which may log technical details such as your IP address to block spam, and it is delivered to our support inbox at Gmail (Google).
  • If you email us, we see your email address and your message.
  • We keep messages only as long as we need them to help you and improve the app. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you and improving TankLog (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
