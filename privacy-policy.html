TankLog Aquascaper

Privacy Policy · Last updated April 2026

Your data never leaves your device

Overview

TankLog Aquascaper is a personal aquarium tracking app built for hobbyists. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data Collection

TankLog does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app does not connect to any external servers or analytics services.

All data you enter — including tank parameters, water test results, feeding logs, animal and plant records, and tank settings — is stored entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data is included in your device's iCloud backup if you have iCloud backup enabled, which is controlled entirely by your device settings.

You can export your data at any time as a JSON file using the Export feature, and re-import it if you ever reinstall the app.

Photos

If you add photos to animal or plant entries, those photos are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere. Photos are compressed automatically to reduce storage size.

Third-Party Services

TankLog does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No SDKs that collect data are included in the app.

Children's Privacy

TankLog does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
